## mohammad-mirhosseini-caltech
Mohammad Mirhosseini | Assistant Professor of Electrical Engineering and Applied Physics | Electrical Engineering | FILTER: ML-relevant faculty only
research: Mirhosseini's research is on the experimental aspects of quantum engineering.
