# CSHL BioAI PhD (found 2026-10-30 by research agent; cshl.edu blocks our fetcher, so verify in a browser)

- New program: BioAI PhD (Biological and Artificial Intelligence), announced 2025-12-04.
  https://www.cshl.edu/phd-program/bioai-phd-program/ ; https://www.cshl.edu/school-of-biological-sciences-launches-bioai-ph-d-program/
- For: dry-lab computational / quantitative work at the AI-biology interface; requires a master's in a quantitative
  field (stats qualifies); no biology background required.
- Differences from the standard (Watson) PhD: direct admission into one lab, no rotations; 3-4 years; ~5-10/yr;
  PI-funded (admission depends on the PI having funding); materials: CV, statement, transcripts, letters,
  faculty mentor preferences; interviews case by case.
- Deadline: rolling review from Dec 15 until filled (portal link "bioai27" -> Fall 2027 cycle).
- **Contact PIs before applying: YES (program page encourages it).** Standard PhD: Dec 1 hard deadline, PI contact not needed.
- Leadership: Justin Kinney, Anthony Zador (co-directors); Zachary Lippman (DGS).
