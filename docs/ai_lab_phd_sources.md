## (d) Practical implications

1. Advisor/lab track record beats school brand: UMass NLP and Cornell/Artzi show non-top-5 labs repeatedly placing students at Google/NVIDIA/Meta. Check each target advisor's alumni page for named lab placements (the pages above show how).
2. For representation-learning / interpretability / neural-data / trustworthy ML, Columbia (theoretical neuroscience), Harvard (Kreiman/Pehlevan -> Anthropic) and Stanford are visible pathways to Anthropic/GDM; the examples are individual people, not rates.
3. Fellowship/residency paths are real: Anthropic Fellows converted ~25-50% to offers; Google Student Researcher is open to current PhD students. Prefer advisors who allow lab internships/part-time visiting roles (Artzi and Neyshabur have GDM ties).
4. Do not assume a top-5 PhD is necessary: roughly two-thirds of the sample is not from MIT/Stanford/Berkeley/CMU/Harvard, but we saw few US non-elite schools at OpenAI/Anthropic specifically; those two labs in the sample are mostly Berkeley/Stanford/Harvard/UT Austin/Toronto.
5. Young advisors: the sample cannot test this; not claimed.

## (e) Caveats

- Sample is ~32 people, selected by what search surfaced; heavy survivorship bias (we see people with public bios) and aggregator bias toward DeepMind and Europe/Canada.
- Join years are mostly missing; many rows may predate 2022 (Roy, Misra, Blukis). The requested 2022-2026 window is only confirmed for Jiang (2026), Liu (2024-25) and the UMass 2023-25 graduates (graduation year, not join year).
- Third-party aggregator data (**[agg]**) not verified against personal homepages. Google search-result summaries (not full pages) support several statements marked above.
- Carnegie/MacroPolo measure NeurIPS authors by country/institution, not frontier-lab hires by PhD school; Carnegie data is self-reported and incomplete.
- Not found: any per-school hiring counts for the named labs; no data for BU, UCF, USF, UCSF, Mount Sinai, Rockefeller, Rutgers, Stony Brook, NJIT, UCSD, UMD, Northeastern, and thin data for JHU, NYU, Duke. Next step if needed: scrape OpenReview/Semantic Scholar affiliations or department placement pages.
